[h1]Rebalance Key Crafting[/h1]

Chest keys in Core Keeper are single-use — one key per locked chest — so crafting them again and again quietly burns through your bars. This mod cuts that cost down to something far more reasonable: by default, every tier chest key costs just a quarter of its vanilla materials to craft.

The keys themselves are untouched. Same keys, same chests, same loot — they are simply cheaper to make, so opening that next vault doesn't mean another trip to the smelter.

[b]Purely a cost tweak.[/b] Keys, chests, drop rates and everything else behave exactly like vanilla. The workbench shows the reduced cost, and that is exactly what gets consumed — no mismatch between what you see and what you pay.

[b]Multiplayer:[/b] install the mod on both the client and the server.

[h2]Settings[/h2]

Open Options → Mod settings in-game to choose the crafting cost (1 ingot, 1/4, 1/2 or vanilla), which keys are affected (the seven tier keys — Copper, Iron, Scarlet, Octarine, Galaxite, Solarite, Relucite — or all craftable keys), and to toggle the mod on or off. [b]Changes apply on the next game restart[/b], not immediately — the menu offers to restart for you.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]

[list]
[*] Mod Settings Menu (required) — hosts the in-game settings screen
[*] CoreLib (required) — a dependency of Mod Settings Menu
[/list]

Steam does not prompt for required items the way mod.io does — subscribe to Mod Settings Menu and CoreLib as well, or the mod will not load.
